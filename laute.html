Mixed vowels: ए (e) and ऐ (ai) combine the throat and the palate; ओ (o) and औ (au) combine the throat and the lips. व sits between the lips and the teeth. ह is made in the throat. Retroflex means the tip of the tongue is curled back. Dental means the tongue touches the upper teeth (not the gum ridge as in German t/d).
